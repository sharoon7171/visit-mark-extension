VisitMark requests the browsing history permission only so that, when you enable “Browsing history” under Detection in the popup, it can ask Chrome whether URLs from links on the current page appear in your history (using the History API, including so highlights can stay in sync when history changes) and treat those links as visited for styling; if you leave that option off, the extension does not use history for highlighting, and history is not sent to any server the publisher operates—it is used on your device solely to decide which links to color.
