VisitMark is a Chrome extension that colors visited links the way you want: a default for all sites, optional per-site colors, and the ability to disable highlighting on specific hosts. Detection can follow Chrome’s native visited state, match against your browsing history (including when history is synced), or both; highlights refresh as you navigate, including single-page applications. Preferences sync through Chrome sync when you use it. Processing stays in the browser, with no separate publisher backend used for highlighting.
